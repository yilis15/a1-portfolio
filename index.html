Games That Stayed With Me

Some games are just fun to play. Others become memories that stick with you for years.

Here are three games that have a special place in my heart. Flip each card to take a peek at my gaming journey!

GAME 01

Hollow Knight

The game that got me hooked on a challenge.

I think Hollow Knight was the first truly challenging game I ever played.

I first tried it on someone else's PS5, and it took me two whole days just to beat one of the early bosses. But when I finally did, the feeling was incredible!

I'd never felt that proud of myself in a game before. After that, I downloaded Hollow Knight on my own PC, and it quickly became one of my favorite games.

🏆 My Hollow Knight Journey

My Achievements

I completed all the in-game achievements and earned the Steel Heart achievement on Steam! Definitely one of my proudest gaming moments!

Favorite Moments

GAME 02

Don't Starve Together

My longest-running gaming obsession.

My story with Don't Starve actually started back in middle school.

During a school break, I asked my mom to buy me the single-player mobile version. I was hooked almost immediately!

I spent so much time watching gameplay videos and looking up tips on the wiki, even though I could barely survive a few days in the game.

When I finally got my own computer in high school, Don't Starve Together was the very first game I installed.

It's still my most-played game to this day, and honestly, I think it's the best multiplayer game to play with family!

🏆 My Don't Starve Together Journey

My Achievements

With over 1,350 hours of playtime, this is easily my most-played game! I've spent countless hours surviving, exploring, and having fun with friends and family.

Favorite Moments

GAME 03

Rain World

Welcome to the food chain. You're near the bottom.

I'd heard so many people say that if you loved Hollow Knight, Ori, and Terraria, you'd probably love Rain World too.

Since I'd already beaten all three, I bought Rain World as soon as I saw it on sale.

Rain World is all about survival. It doesn't matter how many weapons you have or how good you are at moving around.

At the end of the day, you're still just a tiny, squishy, and apparently delicious slugcat! Almost everything out there wants to eat you.

🏆 My Rain World Journey

My Achievements

I've made it through some of Rain World's toughest challenges and collected plenty of memorable moments along the way. Being a tiny slugcat isn't easy!
